Tilapia Growth & Derivatives Analysis

Bioscience Context: For Malaysian GIFT Tilapia, growth follows a Gompertz curve. As an Agro-Tech technologist, you use the First Derivative f'(x) to identify the biological "Inflection Point"—the exact day when growth is fastest. After this point, metabolic efficiency drops, and feed conversion ratios (FCR) worsen.

Growth Function f(t): W(t) = K * exp(-exp(-k * (t - t0)))
Rate of Change f'(t): dW/dt = k * W(t) * ln(K / W(t))
