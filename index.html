Oil Palm Height Growth Analysis

As a future agro-industry practitioner, you must manage the "replanting gap"—the period when older palms (20-25+ years) show declining growth rates and productivity (Li, 2024).

Height Function: f(x) = 15 / (1 + e-0.2(x-10))
Growth Rate (Derivative): f'(x) = [3 * e-0.2(x-10)] / (1 + e-0.2(x-10))2
