SFP Agro-Tech: Growth Rate Analysis

Using Derivatives to Model Malaysian Tenera Oil Palm Biomass

Bioscience Problem: Yield Forecasting

In Agro-product technology, we must predict raw material supply. For Oil Palm (Elaeis guineensis), we use the Logistic Growth Model to determine the Instantaneous Growth Rate. The derivative $f'(x)$ indicates the efficiency of the palm in converting nutrients into biomass at any specific year.

Biomass Function: f(x) = L / (1 + e^-k(x-x0))
Derivative (Rate): f'(x) = k * f(x) * (1 - f(x)/L)
Where L=600kg (Max), k=0.4 (Growth Factor), x0=8 (Midpoint Year)
